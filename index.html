_ Azab-e-Ishq _ ❤️‍🩹

"Behtar shayari ki baat kar raha, Ghalib likhne ki koshish kar raha"
— Yeh sabhi shayari main uske Naam kar raha
(2022) / Shayari 🍂 / "Imran"
1.
Har dard mera uske naam likha hai,
Jo likha dil se, woh kaam likha hai.
2.
Woh mila hi nahi kabhi khwab mein,
Phir bhi har raat uska salaam likha hai.
3.
Na kehna kuch, bas aankhon se samajhna,
Maine har baat ko alfaaz ke bina likha hai.
4.
Tere bin zindagi adhoori si lagi,
Ek kami thi jo har shaam likha hai.
